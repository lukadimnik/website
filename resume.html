Engineering Manager and hands-on technical lead with deep platform engineering experience.

My philosophy is simple: happy engineers are productive engineers. I measure my teams in shipped features and migrations, but I'm proudest of the people outcomes: clarity of direction, psychological safety, and real investment in growth conversations. My last team topped the Culture Amp engagement survey across a ~50-engineer org; my current team has run nine months through a founding period and a domain pivot with zero attrition.

Technically, my work lives where reliability meets velocity: AWS Cognito identity consolidation, Datadog observability, GitHub Actions and Terraform automation, and service extraction from a Java 8 monolith. I pair agentic workflows (Claude Code) with a hands-on Kubernetes homelab to stay sharp on Linux, Docker and GitOps, see the case study below.
